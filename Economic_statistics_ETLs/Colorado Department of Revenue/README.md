# 🗂️ Source Agency and Dataset Details

This suite processes datasets provided by the **Colorado Department of Revenue**. Below are the details of the source agency and the datasets used in this example. 🌟

---

## 🏢 Source Agency

**Colorado Department of Revenue**  
Data and reports are published at:  
🔗 [cdor.colorado.gov/data-and-reports](https://cdor.colorado.gov/data-and-reports)

---

## 📊 Dataset Suite

The datasets used in this example are the **Colorado Department of Revenue's Sales Reports**, available at:  
🔗 [cdor.colorado.gov/retail-sales-reports](https://cdor.colorado.gov/retail-sales-reports)

These include the following reports:

### Sales Reports by:
- **State**: 1 file
- **County**: 1 file
- **City**: 2 files
- **Industry**: 2 files
- **County by Industry**: 2 files
- **City by Industry**: 2 files

---

📌 These datasets provide comprehensive sales information across various geographic and industry-specific categories, forming the foundation for the data requirements for this module. 🚀
